# Delete your AkTalki account

You can delete your AkTalki account and data at any time.

## In the app (fastest)
1. Open **AkTalki** and sign in.
2. Go to **Profile → Safety & account → Delete account**.
3. Type **DELETE** and confirm.

## Without the app
Email **kpk07698@gmail.com** from the email address of your AkTalki account with the subject **"Delete my account"**. We will delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

## What is deleted
- Your login and email address
- Your profile, photos and verification selfie
- Your friends list, friend requests and messages

## What is kept
- Safety reports are kept **without your name** so we can keep the community safe.
- Payment records are kept by **Google Play** as required by law. Cancel any active subscription in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.
