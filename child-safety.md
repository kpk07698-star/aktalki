# AkTalki Child Safety Standards

**Last updated:** 15 November 2026

AkTalki has **zero tolerance** for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sextortion, and any sexualisation of minors.

## Our standards
1. **Adults only.** AkTalki is for people aged 18+. Sign-up requires a date of birth, and our database automatically rejects anyone under 18.
2. **Friendship only.** Romantic or sexual messages are against our rules.
3. **No CSAE or CSAM.** Any content or behaviour that sexualises, exploits or endangers a child is strictly prohibited.
4. **Messaging limits.** Private messages are only possible between accepted friends, and anti-spam limits are enforced.

## How users report concerns
- **In the app:** open any profile or chat → tap **•••** → **Report** → choose **"Might be under 18"** or another reason. Users can also **Block** at any time.
- **By email:** **kpk07698@gmail.com**

## What we do
- We review reports, prioritising any report involving a minor or child safety.
- We remove offending content and permanently ban accounts involved in CSAE.
- We report apparent CSAM to the relevant authorities, including the **National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC)** and/or the appropriate local law enforcement, as required by law.
- We cooperate with law enforcement investigations.
