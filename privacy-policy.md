# AkTalki Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 15 November 2026

AkTalki ("we", "us") is a friendship app for adults (18+) that helps people from different countries make friends. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and the choices you have.

Contact: **kpk07698@gmail.com**

## 1. Information we collect

**You give us:**
- **Account:** email address and password (passwords are stored securely and encrypted by our provider; we never see them).
- **Date of birth:** used only to confirm you are 18 or older. It is never shown to other users.
- **Profile:** name, profile photo, country, languages, interests and "About you" text. Other signed-in members can see these.
- **Messages:** private messages you send to friends.
- **Verification selfie (optional):** if you request a blue tick, your selfie is stored privately and seen only by our safety team.
- **Reports:** if you report someone, the reason and details you write.

**Collected automatically:**
- **Online status:** whether the app is open right now (shown as a green dot to other members).
- **Basic technical data** needed to run the service (for example, sign-in tokens and error logs).

We do **not** collect your precise location, contacts, or microphone recordings, and we do **not** sell your personal information.

## 2. How we use information
- To create and run your account and show your profile to other members.
- To deliver friend requests and messages.
- To keep AkTalki safe: enforcing the 18+ rule, reviewing reports, verifying profiles and preventing spam.
- To process subscriptions (Plus and VIP) through Google Play.
- To answer your support requests.

## 3. Sharing
We share information only with service providers that help us run AkTalki, under contracts that protect it:
- **Supabase** (database, sign-in, file storage and real-time messaging).
- **Google Play** (app distribution and subscription payments; we never see your card details).

We may disclose information if required by law or to protect someone's safety.

## 4. Data retention and deletion
- Your data is kept while your account is active.
- You can **delete your account at any time** in the app: **Profile → Safety & account → Delete account**. This permanently deletes your login, profile, photos, verification selfie, friendships and messages.
- Safety reports are kept without your name so we can keep the community safe.
- You can also request deletion by emailing **kpk07698@gmail.com** from your account's email address.

## 5. Security
Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database rules ensure members can only access what they are allowed to (for example, only the two people in a chat can read it). No system is 100% secure, but we work hard to protect your data.

## 6. Age requirement
AkTalki is **only for people aged 18 or older**. We block sign-ups with a birth date under 18. If you believe someone under 18 is using AkTalki, please report them in the app or email us.

## 7. Your rights
Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data. You can edit your profile in the app at any time, or contact us at **kpk07698@gmail.com**.

## 8. Changes
If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
